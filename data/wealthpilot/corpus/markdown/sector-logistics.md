# Sector Underwriting Note: Logistics

**ASHVA CAPITAL INTERNAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL**
**SECTION 4: INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC UNDERWRITING PROTOCOLS**
**SUB-SECTION 4.12: LOGISTICS AND FREIGHT TRANSPORT SECTOR**

**4.12.1 Purpose and Scope**
This sub-section governs credit evaluations for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) operating within the logistics, warehousing, and freight transport sector. All underwriters must adhere strictly to the quantitative thresholds and qualitative constraints detailed herein. 

**4.12.2 Portfolio Exposure Cap**
(a) Maximum exposure to the logistics sector is capped at **10.0 percent** of Ashva Capital’s total active loan book.
(b) Current Portfolio Status: The logistics sector is currently at its maximum exposure cap of **10.0 percent**.
(c) Processing Directive: All new credit applications originating from the logistics sector shall automatically trigger reason code **SECTOR_CAP**. Automated approval engines are prohibited from issuing binding loan offers under this code. All applications bearing code **SECTOR_CAP** must be routed immediately to a designated human Senior Credit Reviewer for manual portfolio-capacity assessment.

**4.12.3 Working Capital Cycle**
(a) The standard operating working capital cycle for approved logistics SMEs is **30 days**, measured from fuel and maintenance outlay to final invoice settlement by corporate shippers.
(b) Underwriters must verify historical debtor days through bank statements and accounts receivable ledgers. Any applicant demonstrating a working capital cycle exceeding **45 days** requires sign-off from the Head of Credit Risk, Varun Sharma.

**4.12.4 Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) Floor**
(a) Notwithstanding the standard corporate portfolio DSCR floor of 1.25x, the sector-specific DSCR floor for logistics SME lending is **1.40x**.
(b) The **1.40x** floor must be calculated using trailing twelve-month (TTM) net operating income divided by total scheduled debt service, inclusive of equipment lease obligations.

**4.12.5 Loan Tenor and Facility Limits**
(a) The maximum allowable loan tenor for any facility within this sector is **48 months**. Longer tenors are strictly prohibited, regardless of collateral quality.

**4.12.6 Seasonality and Cash Flow Adjustments**
(a) The logistics sector experiences a pronounced seasonal peak running from **September to December** annually, driven by retail inventory build-up and holiday fulfillment demand.
(b) Underwriters must base DSCR calculations on normalized annual cash flows rather than peak-season earnings. 
(c) For borrowers applying during the off-peak trough (February to May), underwriters may apply a seasonal revenue adjustment factor of up to **1.15x** to historical trough months, provided the borrower maintains a minimum unencumbered cash reserve equal to **2 months** of debt service in an Ashva Capital-controlled escrow account.

**4.12.7 Primary Causes of Sector Distress**
Underwriters must explicitly evaluate and document mitigation strategies for the two most common causes of financial distress in the logistics sector:
1. *Volatile Fuel and Energy Costs:* Unhedged spikes in diesel or electricity expenditures that compress operating margins before freight rate adjustments can be passed to end-shippers.
2. *Fleet Maintenance and Capital Obsolescence:* Unexpected mechanical failures or regulatory compliance costs (such as emissions upgrades) that demand immediate, unbudgeted capital expenditure, resulting in sudden liquidity depletion.

**4.12.8 Acceptable Collateral Types**
Collateral must be restricted to the following asset classes, subject to the specified maximum Advance Rates against orderly liquidation value (OLV):
1. *Commercial Freight Vehicles and Prime Movers:* Maximum Advance Rate of **65 percent** of appraised OLV, provided vehicles are aged under **5 years** at origination.
2. *Warehousing and Material Handling Equipment:* Maximum Advance Rate of **50 percent** of appraised OLV.
3. *First-Ranking Charge on Book Debts (Accounts Receivable):* Maximum Advance Rate of **75 percent** against eligible receivables aged under **60 days**.
