# Adverse Action and Applicant Communication Standard

# ASHVA CAPITAL INTERNAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL
## SECTION 14: CREDIT DECLINES, REASON CODES, AND APPEALS
**Document ID:** AC-POL-CR-14  
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2026  
**Applies To:** All Underwriters, Senior Credit Officers, and Credit Operations Staff  

---

### 14.1 Purpose and Scope
This section governs the mandatory protocols for declining commercial loan applications within Ashva Capital. Underwriters are strictly bound by the procedures, timelines, and controlled vocabularies set forth herein. Deviation from these rules constitutes a Tier 2 compliance violation.

---

### 14.2 Turnaround Deadlines
1. **Standard Turnaround:** Every credit application submitted through the Ashva Capital Origination Portal must reach a final underwriting decision—whether approval, referral, or decline—within **48 business hours** of receiving a fully completed application file.
2. **Incomplete Files:** If an application lacks mandatory verification documents, the underwriter must issue an Information Request within **24 business hours**. The applicant has **5 business hours** to cure the deficiency. If the deficiency is not cured, the application must be declined immediately under Section 14.4.14.

---

### 14.3 Mandatory Content of a Decline Notice
Every formal Decline Notice issued to an applicant or their authorized broker must be generated automatically via the Ashva Capital Core System and must contain the following six elements:
1. **Unique Application Identifier** generated at the time of initial submission.
2. **Exact Date and Time** of the decline decision.
3. **Primary Decline Reason Code** selected strictly from the authorized list in Section 14.4.
4. **Factual Basis Summary:** A two-sentence factual explanation derived from the verified underwriting worksheet (e.g., matching the specific financial ratio or bureau metric that breached policy).
5. **Right to Appeal Notice:** Explicit instructions detailing the appeals process, including the mandatory submission window.
6. **Regulatory Grievance Reference:** The contact coordinates for the Ashva Capital Grievance Redressal Cell.

---

### 14.4 Authorized Decline Reason Codes
Underwriters are strictly prohibited from citing any reason for a decline that does not appear in the following exhaustive list. Free-text explanations in decline notices are disabled in the core system.

* **14.4.1 APPROVED_STANDARD:** Meets every underwriting standard (used exclusively when closing an application that was inadvertently routed to the decline queue due to a system rule override).
* **14.4.2 DSCR_BELOW_FLOOR:** Debt-service coverage ratio is below the sector floor.
* **14.4.3 THIN_FILE:** Fewer than 24 months operating, or no active trade line.
* **14.4.4 ADVERSE_BUREAU:** Bureau score below 450.
* **14.4.5 PAST_DELINQUENCY:** A 90-day delinquency, or 500,000 rupees or more written off.
* **14.4.6 DOC_MISMATCH:** Declared revenue differs from verified revenue by more than 10 percent.
* **14.4.7 SECTOR_CAP:** The sector has reached its maximum share of the loan book.
* **14.4.8 LEVERAGE_ABOVE_LIMIT:** Total debt after the loan exceeds 6.0 times EBITDA.
* **14.4.9 LIQUIDITY_BELOW_FLOOR:** Current ratio is below 1.10.
* **14.4.10 TENOR_ABOVE_LIMIT:** Requested tenor is longer than the grade or the sector allows.
* **14.4.11 COLLATERAL_SHORTFALL:** Collateral is worth less than the cover the grade requires.
* **14.4.12 FRAUD_HOLD:** Declared revenue differs from verified revenue by more than 25 percent.
* **14.4.13 GRADE_REQUIRES_REVIEW:** Grade C2 or D1: never approved automatically.
* **14.4.14 INCOMPLETE_APPLICATION:** A figure needed to test a standard was not supplied.
* **14.4.15 WITHDRAWN_BY_APPLICANT:** The applicant withdrew the application.

---

### 14.5 Prohibition on Unapproved Reasons
1. Underwriters, loan officers, and credit committee members are legally and operationally barred from communicating any decline rationale, whether verbally or in writing, that deviates from the exact text and code of Section 14.4.
2. Internal notes must reference the exact code. Any customer communication containing colloquial justifications (such as "poor market outlook" or "insufficient management depth" when not tied to a code) will trigger an audit by the Chief Risk Officer.

---

### 14.6 Appeal Process and Deadlines
1. **Submission Window:** An applicant may appeal a decline decision by submitting a formal Notice of Appeal through the Ashva Capital Portal within **14 calendar days** of the date stamped on the Decline Notice. Appeals received after the 14-day window will be automatically rejected by the system without review.
2. **Mandatory Grounds for Appeal:** An appeal must be accompanied by new, verifiable documentary evidence that directly rectifies the specific decline reason code cited (e.g., corrected tax filings for a `DOC_MISMATCH` or a certified capital injection curing `LIQUIDITY_BELOW_FLOOR`).
3. **Review Authority:** All timely appeals shall be adjudicated by the Independent Credit Appeals Committee (ICAC), comprising the Chief Credit Officer, Head of Risk, and a rotating Senior Underwriter who was not part of the initial credit decision.
4. **Appeal Turnaround:** The ICAC shall render a final, non-appealable decision within **10 business days** of receiving the complete appeal package.

---

### 14.7 Record Retention Period
1. All declined application files, including verified financial statements, bureau reports, automated scoring logs, the issued Decline Notice, and any appeal documentation, must be retained in the Ashva Capital Digital Archives.
2. **Retention Period:** Records shall be stored in an immutable, read-only state for a minimum of **7 years** from the date of the decline notice, or for the duration required by applicable statutory financial regulations, whichever is longer. Destruction of records prior to the expiration of the 7-year period requires joint written authorization from the Chief Credit Officer and the Head of Legal and Compliance.
