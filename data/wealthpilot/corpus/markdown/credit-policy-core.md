# Ashva Capital Credit Policy Manual: Core Underwriting Standards

ASHVA CAPITAL PRIVATE LIMITED  
CREDIT POLICY MANUAL  
DOCUMENT ID: CPM-SME-04  
SECTION 4: CORE UNDERWRITING STANDARDS  

---

### 4.01 Purpose and Scope
This manual establishes the mandatory underwriting standards for Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) term loans administered by Ashva Capital Private Limited. All underwriting personnel, automated decision engines, and delegated committees are bound by the numerical thresholds and classification rules set forth herein. No deviations are permitted except through the formal exception process defined in Section 4.12.

### 4.02 Minimum Operating History
1. The applicant business must demonstrate a continuous minimum operating history of 24 months prior to the date of application.
2. Operating history shall be verified via primary incorporation documents, tax registration certificates, and continuous bank statement analysis covering the full 24-month period.
3. Any application representing a business operating for less than 24 months shall be declined automatically by the underwriting system.

### 4.03 Credit Bureau Scoring and Grading Architecture
1. The underwriting system shall assign a risk grade based on the applicant's primary bureau score in accordance with the following schedule:
   - Grade A1: Minimum bureau score of 750
   - Grade A2: Minimum bureau score of 700
   - Grade B1: Minimum bureau score of 650
   - Grade B2: Minimum bureau score of 600
   - Grade C1: Minimum bureau score of 550
   - Grade C2: Minimum bureau score of 500
   - Grade D1: Minimum bureau score of 450
2. Any primary bureau score below 450 shall be classified as Grade D2 and designated as an adverse bureau record.

### 4.04 Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) Standards
1. Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) is defined as EBITDA divided by total annual debt service.
2. Total annual debt service is calculated as the existing annual debt service plus the first twelve monthly instalments on the proposed loan.
3. The first twelve monthly instalments on the proposed loan shall be calculated on a reducing balance at the assessment rate of 14.00 percent per annum.
4. The minimum allowable DSCR is 1.25x, or the applicable sector floor where the sector floor is higher than 1.25x.
5. Worked DSCR Calculation Example:
   - EBITDA: 2,400,000 rupees
   - Existing annual debt service: 600,000 rupees
   - Proposed loan: 2,000,000 rupees over a 48-month tenor
   - First twelve instalments at the assessment rate of 14.00 percent: 655,835 rupees
   - Total annual debt service = 600,000 + 655,835 = 1,255,835 rupees
   - $\text{DSCR} = \frac{2,400,000}{1,255,835} = 1.91\text{x}$

### 4.05 Leverage and Liquidity Floors
1. Maximum Leverage: Existing total debt plus the proposed loan amount may not exceed 6.0 times EBITDA.
2. Current-Ratio Floor: The applicant’s current ratio (current assets divided by current liabilities) must be at least 1.10.

### 4.06 Delinquency and Adverse Record Definitions
1. A bureau record is defined as adverse if the bureau score is below 450.
2. A past delinquency is defined as any occurrence of a 90-day past-due delinquency within the bureau history, or any written-off amount totaling 500,000 rupees or more.

### 4.07 Application Status Classification
1. Declined Status: An application shall be declined immediately if:
   - The calculated DSCR is below 1.00;
   - The bureau record is adverse;
   - The business has operated for less than 24 months; or
   - The assigned grade is D2.
2. Borderline Status: An application shall be classified as borderline, requiring mandatory human review, if:
   - Any standard other than those triggering an automatic decline is missed;
   - There is a past delinquency present in the credit history;
   - The requested tenor is longer than allowed by the assigned grade or sector limits;
   - The collateral valuation is worth less than the cover required by the grade;
   - Any numerical figure required to test a standard is missing from the file; or
   - The assigned grade is C2 or D1.
3. Approvable Status: An application shall be classified as approvable only when all financial, bureau, and operational standards are fully met without triggering decline or borderline criteria.

### 4.08 Automated vs. Manual Processing Rules
1. The underwriting system may approve an application automatically only if all of the following conditions are met simultaneously:
   - The application status is classified as approvable;
   - The requested loan amount is no more than 1,000,000 rupees; and
   - The assigned grade is C1 or better (A1, A2, B1, B2, or C1).
2. All applications failing to meet all three automated approval conditions—including all applications resulting in a decline or a borderline classification—require human sign-off and cannot be processed straight-through.

### 4.09 Lending Base Rate
1. Ashva Capital's lending base rate is established at 9.50 percent per annum. All risk-adjusted pricing spreads shall be added on top of this base rate in accordance with the credit risk pricing grid maintained by the Risk Management Department.

### 4.10 Loan-Size Tiers and Approval Authorities
1. Loan applications shall be routed to approval authorities based strictly on the total requested principal amount:
   - Tier 1 (Up to 1,000,000 rupees): Credit Officer
   - Tier 2 (Up to 5,000,000 rupees): Credit Committee
   - Tier 3 (Exceeding 5,000,000 rupees): Board Credit Committee
2. Mandatory Signatory Rule: No loan above 5,000,000 rupees may be approved without a named human signatory from the Board Credit Committee. Automated approval is strictly prohibited for any loan exceeding 1,000,000 rupees.

### 4.11 Compliance and Audit Trail
1. All underwriting decisions, whether generated automatically or executed by human reviewers, must be logged in the core lending system with a permanent audit trail recording the input variables, calculated ratios, assigned grades, and identity of the approving officer or committee members.
